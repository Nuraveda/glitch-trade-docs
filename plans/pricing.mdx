## Marketplace revenue split

When authors sell strategies through the marketplace, **Glitch Trade takes 30%, the author keeps 70%**. Pricing is set per strategy (one-time or subscription).

## What we don't sell

- We don't sell strategies (you author your own; the marketplace lists what other users
  publish)
- We don't sell signals
- We don't sell account-management services

Glitch Trade is tooling. You author the automation, decide the broker path, and stay
responsible for execution decisions.
