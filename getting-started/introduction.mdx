Glitch Trade is the platform to **run, test, and track** AI trading automation. Connect a broker, author a strategy in plain English or code, backtest it against historical bars, and deploy it to your account — all from one dashboard.

We don't trade on your behalf. Funds stay at your broker. Glitch Trade is the automation + tracking layer on top of accounts you already own.

## What this is not

Glitch Trade is **not** a signal service, an "AI picks" product, or a guaranteed-returns service. It's tooling for traders who already have ideas and want to execute, test, and track them consistently. The math is real but the edge depends on you.
