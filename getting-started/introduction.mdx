Glitch Executor is a **prop-challenge tracker, strategy builder, and backtester**. Connect your broker, watch every challenge across firms in one dashboard, draft the next strategy in our editor, and pre-flight it against the same firm rules before you fund a real attempt.

We don't trade on your behalf. Funds stay at your broker. Glitch Executor is the visibility + authoring + testing layer on top of accounts you already own.

## What this is not

Glitch Executor is **not** a signal service, an "AI picks" product, a copy-trading platform, or a guaranteed-returns service. It's tooling for traders who already have ideas and want one place to track every challenge, draft the next strategy, and stress-test it against firm rules. The math is real but the edge depends on you.
